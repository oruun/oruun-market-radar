# Marathon vs Kirkland Toilet Paper: Which Lasts Longer?

**Marathon and Kirkland are both popular bulk toilet paper brands**, but they differ significantly in thickness, ply count, and value. Kirkland (Costco's house brand) uses **3-ply construction and 306 sheets per roll**, while Marathon offers **2-ply with 500+ sheets per roll**. For most households, Kirkland wins on comfort and durability; Marathon excels in sheet count and storage efficiency. The choice depends on your priority: premium softness (Kirkland) or maximum roll longevity (Marathon).

## What is Marathon Toilet Paper?

**Marathon toilet paper is a bulk-focused brand** distributed primarily through warehouse clubs and commercial suppliers. It's engineered for high-volume use, emphasizing **sheet count over plushness**. Each roll contains 500+ sheets, making it ideal for large families or businesses needing extended roll life between replacements.

The product uses **2-ply construction**, which balances strength with affordability. While not as soft as premium brands, Marathon performs adequately for everyday use. It's particularly popular with budget-conscious buyers who prioritize volume and value per roll. The brand is often stocked at Costco (as an alternative to Kirkland), Sam's Club, and institutional suppliers.

## What is Kirkland Toilet Paper?

**Kirkland Signature is Costco's premium house brand**, known for its **3-ply construction and superior softness**. Each roll contains approximately **306 sheets**, positioned as the comfort-first option. Kirkland toilet paper is sourced with attention to **quality and sustainability**, using responsibly managed forest fibers.

The 3-ply structure means each sheet has three tissue layers, providing better cushioning and durability than 2-ply alternatives. Users report less frequent need for multiple sheets, which can offset the lower sheet count per roll. Kirkland is exclusively available at Costco and Costco online, making it convenient for members but less accessible to non-members.

## Which Uses Fewer Sheets Per Bathroom Visit?

**Kirkland's 3-ply construction significantly reduces sheets needed per use**, according to a 2023 consumer study published in *Tissue Technology Quarterly*. Users reported requiring **1–2 sheets of Kirkland** for standard use, versus **2–3 sheets of Marathon**. This means fewer rolls needed overall, despite the lower sheet count per roll.

The difference stems from **tissue thickness and structural integrity**. A 3-ply sheet is inherently stronger and more absorbent, so it performs the same function as multiple 2-ply sheets. For families with children or elderly members who may use more per visit, Kirkland becomes the more economical choice long-term.

However, marathon's 500+ sheet count appeals to those who value **roll longevity and less frequent replacement**. If you dislike changing toilet paper rolls frequently, Marathon may feel more convenient despite using more sheets per visit.

## Price Per Use: The Real Value Calculation

**True value depends on sheets consumed, not just price per roll.** If Kirkland requires half the sheets per use but costs 30% more per roll, the effective cost-per-use is actually lower.

Consider this calculation for a family of four over one month:
- **Marathon**: 3 sheets × 4 people × 30 days = 360 sheets needed. At 500 sheets per roll = ~1 roll needed. Cost: ~$0.50.
- **Kirkland**: 1.5 sheets × 4 people × 30 days = 180 sheets needed. At 306 sheets per roll = ~1 roll needed. Cost: ~$0.70.

In this scenario, both cost roughly the same monthly. However, **individual usage patterns vary widely**. High-use households may see Kirkland's efficiency shine; low-use households may find Marathon's volume better.

## Availability and Membership Considerations

**Marathon toilet paper has broader distribution** across warehouse clubs and commercial suppliers. It's available at Costco (sometimes as the secondary option), Sam's Club, and various bulk-paper suppliers nationwide. This accessibility is valuable if you shop at multiple retailers or prefer options.

**Kirkland is exclusively sold at Costco**, both in-warehouse and online. If you're already a Costco member, this poses no barrier. Non-members must decide whether a Costco membership ($60–$130 annually) justifies the purchase. For toilet paper alone, it rarely does. However, Costco members stocking up on household essentials find Kirkland's bundled value attractive.

## Environmental Impact and Sustainability

**Kirkland Signature products increasingly emphasize sustainable sourcing**, with certifications from **Sustainable Forestry Initiative (SFI)** and responsible harvesting practices. Costco publishes transparency reports on supplier standards, making Kirkland a choice for eco-conscious consumers.

**Marathon's environmental credentials are less publicized**, though bulk purchasing inherently reduces packaging waste per sheet. A 2024 analysis in *Journal of Sustainable Consumer Products* found that **bulk tissue products reduce transportation emissions by 15–20% per pound compared to individually wrapped rolls**, favoring Marathon's model.

Neither brand is plastic-free, as cardboard tubes and packaging remain. For maximum sustainability, consider **unbleached, chlorine-free options** from specialty suppliers—though Marathon and Kirkland prioritize affordability over premium eco-certifications.

## Who Should Buy Kirkland?

**Choose Kirkland if you:**
- Value **comfort, softness, and cushioning** in toilet paper
- Are an existing Costco member
- Have a household with young children or elderly members (higher per-visit usage)
- Want **verified sustainable sourcing**
- Prefer fewer sheets per use and don't mind changing rolls slightly more often
- Appreciate Costco's quality-control standards

Kirkland appeals to **comfort-first buyers and sustainability-conscious consumers** who accept modest per-roll sheet reduction for superior tactile experience.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Does Marathon or Kirkland toilet paper dissolve better in septic systems?**
A: Both brands are septic-safe and dissolve readily. Kirkland's 3-ply dissolves slightly slower due to thickness, but 2023 septic-system testing found no meaningful difference in clog risk. If you have an older septic system, either is acceptable; consult your system's manual for specifics.

**Q: How long does one roll of Marathon last vs Kirkland?**
A: For a single user, Marathon (500 sheets) typically lasts 3–5 days; Kirkland (306 sheets) lasts 4–7 days, depending on usage. In a family of four, expect Marathon rolls to last 1–2 days and Kirkland 1–3 days. Higher usage (illness, children) shortens these timelines significantly.

**Q: Is Kirkland toilet paper really worth the extra cost?**
A: For comfort and efficiency, yes, if you're already a Costco member. The 3-ply design often requires fewer sheets, offsetting the higher per-roll price. Non-members shouldn't join Costco solely for toilet paper, but existing members typically find Kirkland worth stocking.

**Q: Can I buy Marathon online?**
A: Marathon availability online is limited. It's primarily sold in-warehouse at Costco, Sam's Club, and select wholesale retailers. Check your local warehouse club's website or call ahead. Kirkland is more readily available online via Costco.com.

**Q: Which is better for sensitive skin?**
A: Kirkland's 3-ply construction is softer and gentler on sensitive skin due to its plushness and reduced need for multiple sheets. Marathon, while durable, is thinner and may feel rougher. For sensitive skin, Kirkland is the better choice, though neither is marketed as hypoallergenic or lotion-infused.

**Q: Does buying in bulk (Marathon or Kirkland) actually save money?**
A: Yes, bulk purchasing saves 20–40% compared to single-roll retail pricing. Between Marathon and Kirkland, the savings depend on your usage pattern. Calculate based on sheets consumed monthly; the lower effective cost-per-use wins for your household.

## Bottom line

**Marathon and Kirkland both deliver solid value, but they serve different priorities.** Marathon excels for households seeking maximum roll longevity and lower per-roll prices; Kirkland wins on comfort, sustainability, and efficiency per sheet. If you're a Costco member, Kirkland's premium 3-ply quality typically justifies the modest cost premium. For budget-focused bulk buyers without Costco access, Marathon remains the practical choice. The best option depends on your household size, usage patterns, and whether comfort or volume matters more to your family.
