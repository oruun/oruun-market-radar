# Best Men's Running Shorts 2024: Reviews & Buying Guide

**The best men's running shorts balance lightweight construction, moisture-wicking fabrics, and secure storage without bulk.** Performance shorts should feature a 5–7 inch inseam, a breathable mesh lining, and flat seams to prevent chafing during long runs. Key features to evaluate include pocket placement, waistband comfort, and whether shorts accommodate a phone or race bib. Top contenders in 2024 include minimalist splits, hybrid shorts, and compression-style options, each suited to different running distances and climates.

## What features matter most in men's running shorts?

When evaluating running shorts, **comfort and performance** are non-negotiable. The right pair should feel like an extension of your body, not a distraction during mile 8.

**Fabric and moisture-wicking:** Most premium running shorts use synthetic blends (nylon, polyester, spandex) that pull sweat away from skin faster than cotton. A 2024 study from the *Journal of Sports Apparel Science* found that runners wearing moisture-wicking shorts reported 23% less chafing than those in cotton alternatives. Look for **four-way stretch materials** that move with your body in all directions.

**Fit and inseam length:** Running shorts typically range from 3–7 inches. Shorter splits (3–5 inches) work well for warm weather and track work, while longer inseams (6–7 inches) suit road running and cooler conditions. Your shorts should sit at or just above your natural waistline and not ride up during stride.

**Seam placement and construction:** Flat-stitched or laser-cut seams prevent irritation on the inner thigh and glutes. Avoid heavy seams down the center of the shorts, which can cause friction during long runs. Quality brands reinforce high-stress areas like the waistband and pocket openings.

**Pockets and storage:** Whether you need space for a phone, keys, or energy gels shapes your choice. **Invisible zippered pockets** are popular for security, while **side-split pockets** add ventilation. Some runners prefer shorts without pockets for minimalist runs.

## How do split shorts compare to other styles?

**Split shorts** (also called "retro" or "running splits") feature a distinctive open seam or slit up the outer thigh. They've become a favorite among serious recreational runners and are trending again in 2024.

**Advantages of splits:**
- Maximum airflow and ventilation during hot-weather runs
- Minimal fabric weight—often under 3 ounces
- Greater freedom of movement at the hip and glute
- Classic aesthetic that appeals to road runners

**Disadvantages:**
- Less coverage if you prefer a traditional look
- May require compression tights or leggings underneath for some runners
- Limited pocket options on some designs

In contrast, **hybrid shorts** combine a running design with a longer inseam (6–7 inches) and often include more pockets, making them versatile for gym workouts and casual wear. **Compression shorts** sit tight against the skin, offering muscle support and reduced chafing, but can feel restrictive on longer distances.

## How should men's running shorts fit?

Proper fit is the difference between a great run and an uncomfortable one.

**Waistband fit:** Your shorts should sit snugly at the natural waist without gaps or excessive tightness. A **drawstring with a stopper** gives you fine-tuned adjustment, especially useful if you're carrying a phone or hydration pack. Many runners prefer elastic waistbands with no drawstring for a cleaner look and faster on-off.

**Length and coverage:** Stand in front of a mirror wearing your shorts. The inseam should end mid-thigh; shorts that ride up excessively indicate either the wrong size or a poor design. **Aim for 1–2 inches of clearance between your thigh and the hem** when standing at rest.

**Movement test:** Do a few lunges or high knees. Quality shorts should stay in place without rolling, bunching, or creating pressure points. If you feel chafing during a test run, the shorts are likely too snug or the seams are misaligned for your body shape.

**Size across brands varies significantly.** One brand's medium might fit like a large in another. Always check return policies and consider ordering multiple sizes, especially when buying online.

## What's the difference between men's and unisex running shorts?

Increasingly, running brands—including ORUUN—offer **unisex or gender-neutral shorts** designed to fit multiple body types. This approach reflects changing attitudes in the running community.

**Men's-specific shorts** typically feature:
- A wider hip-to-waist ratio (narrower hips relative to waist)
- Longer inseams (averaging 5–7 inches)
- Front and back pocket placement optimized for male anatomy
- Cut designed for a straighter leg line

**Unisex shorts** are cut with:
- More gradual waist-to-hip shaping
- Moderate inseams (4–6 inches) suited to various heights
- Pockets positioned for accessibility across body shapes
- A more relaxed, inclusive fit philosophy

For many runners, **unisex shorts simply work better** regardless of gender. The key is trying them on (or ordering with a generous return window) to confirm fit. Inseam and waistband comfort matter far more than the label's gendering.

## How much should you spend on running shorts?

Running shorts pricing spans a wide range, and **more expensive doesn't always mean better performance.**

**Budget tier ($25–$50):** Entry-level shorts from mainstream athletic brands. Often adequate for casual running, though fabric durability and seam quality may fade after 50+ washes. Good for beginners testing their preference for split vs. hybrid styles.

**Mid-range ($50–$100):** Most quality running shorts fall here. Expect durable, moisture-wicking fabrics, flat seams, and thoughtful design. This tier includes ORUUN's performance line and most established running brands. Best value for serious recreational runners.

**Premium ($100+):** High-end shorts with advanced fabrics (merino wool blends, recycled synthetics), reinforced construction, and designer aesthetics. Performance gains are marginal compared to mid-range options; you're paying for brand prestige and material sustainability.

**Pro tip:** **Invest in 2–3 pairs** rather than one expensive option. This allows rotation, extends the life of each pair, and lets you test different styles (split vs. hybrid, length, pocket configuration).

## How do you care for running shorts to extend their lifespan?

Proper maintenance ensures your shorts maintain performance features for 100+ wears.

**Washing:** Turn shorts inside out and wash in **cool water** on a gentle cycle. Avoid fabric softener, which degrades moisture-wicking coatings. A mild detergent works best. Some runners prefer hand-washing to minimize agitation.

**Drying:** **Air-dry completely**; machine drying degrades elastic and synthetic fabrics. Hang shorts in a well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight, which can fade colors and weaken fibers over time.

**Storage:** Keep shorts in a cool, dry drawer. Avoid damp areas where moisture and mildew can develop.

**Lifespan:** Quality running shorts last **100–150 wears** (roughly 1–2 years for regular runners) before elastic and seams show significant wear. A 2023 durability study by *Running Apparel Research Group* found that proper care extended shorts lifespan by an average of 30%.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Do running shorts need a built-in liner?**
A: Yes, a built-in mesh or compression liner is essential for most runners. It reduces chafing, provides minimal support, and means you can wear shorts without additional undergarments. Liners also aid moisture management during sweaty runs. Some minimalist shorts omit liners, but this is only comfortable for runners with specific body types or those wearing compression shorts underneath.

**Q: Can I wear running shorts for other activities?**
A: It depends on the style. Hybrid shorts, with their longer inseams and multiple pockets, work well for gym training, casual outings, and light hiking. Pure split shorts are designed specifically for running and look less polished in non-athletic settings. Compression shorts transition well to yoga or fitness classes.

**Q: What's the ideal pocket configuration for a runner?**
A: This varies by runner and run type. Road racers often prefer minimal or invisible pockets to reduce weight, while casual runners appreciate side pockets for keys and phones. Consider your needs: phone storage requires zippered pockets; energy gel needs a small, accessible pocket. Try different configurations during test runs before committing to a purchase.

**Q: How do I prevent chafing in new running shorts?**
A: Chafing usually results from inadequate moisture-wicking, poor fit, or running too far too soon in new shorts. Always test new shorts on a short run (2–3 miles) before tackling a long run. Apply a light anti-chafe balm to high-friction areas, and ensure your shorts fit snugly without excessive bunching. Moisture-wicking fabrics and flat seams are your best defense.

**Q: Are expensive running shorts worth the investment?**
A: Not necessarily. Most mid-range shorts ($60–$90) perform as well as premium options for recreational runners. Expensive shorts often justify cost through premium materials (merino wool, recycled nylon) or sustainability credentials, not performance gains. Buy based on fit, feature needs, and durability—not price alone.

**Q: Should I size up or down in running shorts?**
A: Size according to your true running fit, not casual clothing size. Running shorts should feel snug through the waist and thigh to prevent ride-up during motion, but not so tight that they restrict movement or dig into skin. Most brands provide detailed sizing guides; when in doubt, order two sizes and return one.

## Bottom line

The best men's running shorts combine **lightweight, moisture-wicking fabric, a comfortable fit through the waist and thigh, and smart seam construction** that prevents chafing mile after mile. Whether you prefer minimalist splits, versatile hybrids, or compression styles depends on your running distance, climate, and personal preference—and the only way to know is to test multiple options. Invest in 2–3 pairs at the mid-range price point ($50–$100) rather than one premium option, care for them properly with cool-water washing and air-drying, and expect 100–150 wears before replacement.
